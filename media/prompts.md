# Media prompts

Every AI-generated image, animation, video, audio overview or infographic on this site is listed here with the prompt and settings that produced it, so each one can be traced and regenerated. Style rules and templates for new media are in the author's media style kit; every item carries a visible "AI-generated" label where it appears.

## Home page hero — `media/home-hero-1600.webp`, `media/home-hero-800.webp`

- **Tool:** Gemini app (image generation). Model version not recorded.
- **Created:** 16 October 2026, before the media style kit existed. Gemini had looked at the site as it was before the 17 October redesign.
- **Request (Chuhee):** "Can you suggest any images for the site? These can be user for Illustrations across the site"
- **Gemini's description of what it made (its own words, abridged):** an editorial-style hero illustration, "a textured, modern editorial illustration", using the site's palette of "deep teals, slate blues, rust/orange accents, and warm sand grays", showing "people moving (by train, plane, and transit), their attention engaged in digital media and reading, and the overlapping macro forces and pathways that connect Mobility, Travel, and Entertainment."
- **Exact image prompt:** not shown by Gemini; Gemini wrote it internally from the request above.
- **Edits:** none to the picture. Converted from the original 2576 × 1438 JPEG to WebP at 1600 and 800 px wide; on phones the page crops it to 4:3 around the rider.
- **Label on the page:** "AI-generated illustration (Gemini)."
- **Also used in:** the home page social preview, `og-image.png` (1200 × 630), with the label "Illustration: AI-generated (Gemini)".
- **Alt text:** "Illustration: a woman reads a tablet in a train seat by the window; beyond the window, layers of landscape, map grids, a train, a bus and a plane flow past."
