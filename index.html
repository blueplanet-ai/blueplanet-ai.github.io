How these reports are made

These are personal research projects. The views are the author's own and do not represent those of any employer or organization. No company covered sponsors, reviews or endorses this work.

Research, source verification and drafting are carried out with Claude (Anthropic), an AI model; topic selection, scores and conclusions are the author’s. Facts are sourced to official company material first, then recognized benchmark studies, then specialist trade press, and every report lists its references. Each report carries a change log and a next-refresh date, and corrections are welcome via LinkedIn.
