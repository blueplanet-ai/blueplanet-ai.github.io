Time and attention on the move

Every day, people move: to work, to visit, to travel. Along the way they read, watch, learn, work or rest. Mobility, travel and what people do with that time are one connected space. This site traces the forces shaping it and the opportunities it opens.

About

Author and method

Chuhee Lee has spent more than two decades in Silicon Valley turning ideas into products. His work spans the software-defined vehicle, artificial intelligence, autonomous driving and in-vehicle user experience. He leads a multidisciplinary organization of engineers and designers in advanced automotive R&D, including responsibility for portfolio strategy, strategic partnerships and technology trend analysis. He works with startups on proofs of concept and with academic partners on framing research problems so the results can be used in the real world. He holds an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from Johns Hopkins University.

These are personal research projects. The views are the author's own and do not represent those of any employer or organization. No company covered sponsors, reviews or endorses this work.

Research, source verification and drafting are carried out with several AI models, including Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google) and ChatGPT (OpenAI); topic selection, scores and conclusions are the author’s. Facts are sourced to official and primary sources first, then recognized studies, then specialist trade press, and every report lists its references. Each report carries a change log and a next-refresh date, and corrections are welcome via LinkedIn.
