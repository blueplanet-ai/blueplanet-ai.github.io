Summary

The short version

Twenty-one in-car assistants across established automakers, Chinese EV makers and the supplier layer, checked against official sources as of 11 October 2026. Three findings stand out; the full reasoning follows in the strategic read below.

Conversation is commodity. Vehicle control is not.

Every entry understands natural language. The spread is in actuation — Li Auto says nearly all vehicle functions are now voice-controllable, and Tesla's Grok reached 116 of 170 commands in a community-compiled test. That depth needs signal-level access a Gemini or Alexa integration doesn't have on its own.

The assistant has decoupled from the vehicle generation.

GM pushed Gemini over the air to about four million MY2022+ vehicles; Volvo to 16 models back to 2020; Honda to Google built-in trims back to MY2023; Ford offers it as an opt-in. An assistant no longer has to wait for the next electrical architecture — it can reach cars already on the road.

Who owns the stack is now a strategic choice.

The field splits into five architectures, from fully in-house (Tesla, Rivian, Li Auto) to big-tech platforms (GM, Volvo, Honda) to supplier stacks (Huawei, Cerence and SoundHound customers). The highest-scoring cockpit, Huawei's at 4.7, is the one its automaker partners control least. GM and Ford now pair a big-tech assistant with their own vehicle-knowledge agent, and on-device AI has started shipping, making offline competence the next differentiator.

Chapter 1 of 5

Ranked by consequence

The strategic read

Conversation has converged across the field; the measurable spread is in how much of the car the assistant can operate.

01

Conversation has converged. Vehicle control has not.

Every entry on this list understands natural language, holds a multi-turn thread and answers open-domain questions — a capability bought from a handful of model vendors. The measurable spread is in how much of the car the assistant can operate. Li Auto's official launch material says nearly all vehicle functions, down to vents and wipers, are voice-controllable; Grok reached 116 of 170 commands in a community-compiled test; Hyundai's Gleo AI identifies which seat the speaker is in before acting. Platform integrations answer beautifully but reach the vehicle only as far as the OEM exposes its signals to them.

ImplicationDifferentiation budget belongs on the actuation layer and the signal catalogue, not on conversational quality you can license.

02

The assistant has decoupled from the vehicle generation.

GM pushed Gemini to about four million MY2022-and-newer vehicles over the air from April 2026; Volvo followed on 30 April across 16 models dating back to 2020; Honda in July for Google built-in trims back to MY2023; Ford offers Gemini as an opt-in alternative to Google Assistant. Capability is no longer gated by the model year, the head-unit SoC or the E/E generation that shipped with the car.

ImplicationA programme that waits for the next E/E architecture to launch its assistant arrives several model years after the field.

03

Platform assistants are becoming the bridge, not the destination.

GM and Ford now run the same dual track. GM ships Gemini today and confirmed on 31 July that its own assistant, built with an unnamed LLM provider on OnStar and vehicle data, arrives later in 2026. Ford's AI Assistant — hosted on Google Cloud and built on off-the-shelf LLMs — is live in the app for about eight million MY2021+ vehicles and moves into the cabin in 2027, while Gemini handles general voice tasks. The interaction log, the vehicle-health relationship and the service upsell are what both are reclaiming.

ImplicationLicense the general assistant if it saves time, but keep the vehicle-knowledge agent and its data on your side of the boundary.

04

The edge has started shipping.

Rivian's RivianOS 2 (4 September 2026) brought “hybrid edge compute and local AI processing” to R2 and Gen 2 R1; Rivian says R2's 200 sparse TOPS of cabin compute lets the assistant run complex tasks offline. Mercedes signed a multi-year on-device partnership with Liquid AI for third- and fourth-generation MBUX in North America. VW China says personal information in its CEA agents “never leaves the vehicle.” SoundHound announced OASYS Edge in September 2026 — fully embedded agentic voice AI that keeps working offline, deploying from late 2026 — and Cerence xUI and Amazon's Alexa Custom Assistant on NVIDIA DRIVE AGX (automaker evaluation early 2027) are further supplier versions of the same bet.

ImplicationOffline competence and on-board privacy are becoming table stakes. The cloud-only generative path is now the laggard position.

05

China has moved from chat to agents — and the supplier often owns them.

At Auto China 2026 the story was agentic AI that executes tasks: Geely's “Eva” super-agent, BMW's three Qwen-based agents for China, VW's full-domain agent roadmap. Alibaba says nine groups, SAIC Volkswagen among them, will put Qwen-powered in-cabin services into select models. Huawei's HarmonySpace 6 bundles its Celia agent, cabin sensing and phone-to-home continuity for 13 models across five partner brands — and J.D. Power's 2026 China study ranked Huawei-partnered AITO top among premium NEVs. The same study found 48% of complaints were about technology instability and inaccuracy.

ImplicationThe highest-capability cockpit on this list is also the one where the badge-holder owns the least — and rollout speed is outrunning reliability.

06

Owners keep rewarding restraint.

J.D. Power's 2026 U.S. Tech Experience Index (20 August) found owners rate highest the technologies that work quietly in the background — smart ignition drew just 2.4 problems per 100 vehicles — while passenger screens drew 21.3, and 35% of owners who have one never use it. The study did not score voice assistants directly.

ImplicationScore the assistant on tasks completed without the driver looking at a screen, not on demo-able breadth.

Architecture archetypes

How the field is built

Five architectures, each buying something and costing something: vertically integrated, platform-embedded, supplier-integrated, orchestrated multi-agent and in-house pre-generative.

Vertically integrated

TESLA · RIVIAN · HYUNDAI GROUP · VW GROUP CHINA · NIO · LI AUTO · XPENG · XIAOMI

The manufacturer owns or commissions the model layer and wires it directly to vehicle signals. Rivian builds its own assistant and agent framework; Li Auto runs its proprietary MindGPT.

BuysDeepest vehicle control, the interaction data, and a surface to monetize.
CostsA standing model-operations organization and a permanent race against consumer AI.

Platform-embedded

GM · FORD · HONDA / ACURA · VOLVO · BMW

A big-tech assistant becomes the assistant. Gemini replaces Google Assistant inside Google built-in cars; BMW builds its Intelligent Personal Assistant on Alexa+ architecture. GM and Ford now pair this with their own vehicle-knowledge assistants.

BuysFrontier capability on the platform's release cadence, fleet-wide, for little engineering.
CostsThe account, the data and the roadmap. Differentiation narrows to voice, wake word and trim.

Supplier-integrated

VOLKSWAGEN · AUDI · STELLANTIS · LUCID · GEELY · HUAWEI HIMA

A tier-one delivers a branded assistant that routes to a frontier model behind it — Cerence Chat Pro and xUI, SoundHound Chat AI. Huawei is the extreme case: agent, OS, silicon and app store from one vendor.

BuysBrand-owned voice, multi-market localization, and a hedge against any single model vendor.
CostsA capability ceiling set by the supplier's release train, and thin data rights at the far end.

Orchestrated multi-agent

MERCEDES-BENZ

The manufacturer keeps the router and the HMI and dispatches each request to whichever third-party brain answers it best — Google's Automotive AI Agent for places and traffic, other models for open knowledge.

BuysBest-of-breed per task, vendor substitutability, and ownership of the layer the customer experiences.
CostsRouting quality becomes the product, and cloud dependencies degrade together when coverage drops.

In-house, pre-generative

TOYOTA / LEXUS

The OEM builds its own voice agent for commands and natural-language prompts, but has not shipped an LLM assistant in the cockpit. Toyota's agent is built by Toyota Connected North America, with speech recognition from Google Cloud.

BuysControl, embedded speed and offline reliability, with none of the hallucination risk.
CostsA widening capability gap, and a catch-up that likely means skipping a generation.

5
Dated watch list

What moves next

A dated watch list, from VW Group China’s agents and BMW’s Alexa+-based assistant to GM’s own assistant alongside Gemini.

H2 2026

VW Group China's CEA agents confirmed in customer cars

“Agentic AI for all” begins on China Electronic Architecture vehicles — ID. AURA T6, ID. UNYX 09, the Jetta brand and Audi E7X — with CEA 2.0 unifying cockpit and driving in 2027. The first CEA model, the ID. AURA T6, launched on 20 September 2026; whether its agent shipped at launch is not yet confirmed. A test of whether a global automaker's own agent can match local Chinese competitors.

H2 2026

BMW's Alexa+-based assistant spreads beyond the iX3

Live in iX3s in Germany since spring 2026, with US deliveries under way since September. Further Operating System 9 and X models follow from H2 2026 — the test of whether a manufacturer-branded assistant on a big-tech architecture reads to customers as the manufacturer's or as Amazon's.

Late 2026

GM's own assistant arrives alongside Gemini

Confirmed on 31 July: deeper access to vehicle data and telematics, predictive maintenance, owner-specific routines. The first large-scale test of moving from platform-embedded to owned, on vehicles already in customers' hands.

Late 2026

SoundHound OASYS Edge reaches its first deployments

Fully embedded agentic voice AI that keeps working without a connection, announced in September 2026 for deployment from late 2026 and a CES 2027 showing. No automaker customers named yet.

2027

Ford's AI Assistant moves into the cabin; VW's SSP cockpit starts

Ford embeds its vehicle-knowledge assistant in select vehicles. Volkswagen's Qualcomm-based cockpit for the Rivian-JV architecture starts with the ID.EVERY1, promising “agentic AI-driven experiences” in the Western hemisphere.

Early 2027

Alexa Custom Assistant on NVIDIA DRIVE AGX opens for evaluation

Edge speech, vision and language inference in the vehicle with cloud for breadth — the reference supplier-grade hybrid architecture if the latency and privacy story holds.

Q1 2027

Huawei HarmonySpace “Active Suggestion 2.0”

Huawei's OTA roadmap moves the cabin “from passive response to proactive service”, with Active Suggestion 2.0 scheduled for Q1 2027 (Gasgoo).

H1 2027

Mercedes C-Class brings the three-model router to US dealers

Watch the failure modes rather than the demo: how the router discloses which brain answered, and how it behaves out of coverage now that Liquid AI's on-device models are in the plan.

Open

Whether Rivian licenses its assistant beyond its own vehicles

Rivian's CEO said in December 2025 that the UI and its embedded AI are outside the JV; its CSO said in May 2026 that discussions are under way on “similar technologies for the Volkswagen Group.” A deal would make Rivian's stack the first in-house assistant licensed to another automaker.

How the scores were set. Each entry was scored 1–5 per dimension from official manufacturer and supplier material first, then J.D. Power benchmark studies, then specialist trade press, current to 11 October 2026. These are analyst judgements calibrated across the set, not measured results — no vehicle was instrumented or driven. Pre-launch entries (marked Launching) are scored on announced specification, which tends to overstate. Where no official English source could be retrieved, the row says so in its detail. Chinese-market rows rest mainly on trade press and remain the least certain. Readers using this benchmark to inform decisions should validate it with hands-on testing against the seven dimensions.
